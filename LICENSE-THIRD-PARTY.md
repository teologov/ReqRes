### DB-IP IP to Country Lite - Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International

IP Geolocation by [DB-IP (db-ip.com)](https://db-ip.com), CC BY 4.0.

The Destinations window places each captured request's server address in a country using the DB-IP IP to Country Lite database (https://db-ip.com/db/download/ip-to-country-lite), which is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The database has been **modified** for use in ReqRes: the published CSV is converted into a compact binary table by the build script in this repository (`Scripts/geo/build-geo-resources.py`), alongside the two public-domain data sets below. The lookup runs entirely inside the app; no address is ever sent to DB-IP or anywhere else. The map the results are drawn on is Apple Maps (below), which is the one part of the window that talks to a server.

### Apple Maps - MapKit

The map itself (the tiles, coastlines, borders and place names behind the dots) is Apple Maps, shown through Apple's MapKit framework, a part of macOS; nothing of it is bundled with ReqRes. Map data and tiles are © Apple Inc. and its data providers, and the map shows its own legal notice and attribution in its corner. To draw the map, Apple receives the area of the map being viewed, which ReqRes frames around the destinations, but never the addresses ReqRes captured, which are placed from the offline DB-IP table above. The tiles are fetched by macOS's own map service (geod); while capture is running they pass through ReqRes untouched and are not recorded: `*.ls.apple.com` is excluded from interception by default. Use of the map is subject to the Apple Maps terms of use (https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/maps/terms-en.html).

### Natural Earth - Public Domain

The world outlines and country centroids are derived from Natural Earth (https://www.naturalearthdata.com) 1:110m Admin 0 – Countries, which is in the public domain (https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). The outlines are simplified and converted by the same build script; Natural Earth asks for no credit and gets one anyway.

### IANA Time Zone Database - Public Domain

The approximate "You" marker is the country your Mac's time zone belongs to, read from the IANA Time Zone Database (https://www.iana.org/time-zones) files `zone.tab` and `backward`, which are in the public domain. Location Services are never asked.
